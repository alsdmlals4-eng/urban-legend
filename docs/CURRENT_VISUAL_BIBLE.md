## Identity

`SOFT_ANIME_NOIR_LOCKED`: grounded modern Korean urban occult, restrained anime treatment, hand-drawn archival/ink texture, charcoal-blue night, sparse warm amber, and a controlled crimson anomaly accent. The dossier language owns hierarchy and framing, not a competing pixel or sci-fi medium.

### Approved reference fidelity

The supplied record/manual and recovery references establish the fidelity target: near-black archival panels, fine muted-gold rules and seals, warm paper only where a document surface needs it, restrained crimson for anomaly/danger, and illustrated wet environments behind rather than over the information hierarchy. The pale parchment treatment is a Manual/record-surface variant, not the global replacement for the dark institutional base. Do not mix dense illustrated scenery into readable panels or bake panel copy into runtime artwork.

## Screen grammar

- Dynamic copy, buttons, selection, warning, focus, route lines, and HUD are Godot controls/text/procedural drawing—not baked artwork.
- Backgrounds provide location; anomaly overlays stay independently composited; foreground UI must never cover the telegraph, protection target, or evidence read.
- Avoid poster characters, answer-revealing arrows, glossy neon, dense HUD, gore, full-pixel conversion, and photoreal glare.
